    Healthy eating tips

    💧 Drink water

    Drink regularly through the day. Thirst can feel like hunger, so try a glass of water before you snack.

    🥦 Half a plate of vegetables

    Fill half your plate with vegetables or salad. They add fibre and nutrients for few calories.

    🍗 Protein at every meal

    Beans, lentils, tofu, eggs, fish and lean meat keep you full and help maintain muscle.

    🐢 Steady progress

    Losing about 0.5–1 kg (1–2 lb) a week is sustainable. Crash diets rarely last.

    😴 Sleep and move

    Good sleep and daily movement, even walking, make your diet easier to stick to.

    🌍 Eat what you enjoy

    Swap any meal for a local dish with similar calories. A plan you enjoy is a plan you'll follow.
